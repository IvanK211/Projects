# Security reporting narrative outline

This is a presentation plan, not a recovered corporate slide deck. No organization template, brand or business result is included.

## Executive decision

State the reporting period, decision needed and the limits of the evidence. Show known values and missing coverage separately. A synthetic demonstration must say so on every material page.

## Exposure and response

For each metric, show definition, source type, extraction time, denominator, aggregation method, current value, comparison period and owner of interpretation. Separate stock measures from flow measures. A count of blocked events alone does not prove risk reduction.

## People and identity

Distinguish phishing campaign mean from exposure-weighted rate, overdue training from not assigned, PIM request from successful activation and activation from a privileged action. Do not display individual employee performance publicly.

## Reliability and limitations

Explain missing data, retention boundaries, changes in population, schema changes, duplicate handling and incomplete historical coverage. Do not create a green status merely because a system returned no records.

## Action register

Use action, rationale, owner role, due window, dependency, completion evidence and review status. Real owner identities and dates belong only in a private operational deck. Have a human reviewer check both numerical accuracy and whether the narrative is supported.
