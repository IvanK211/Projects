# Controlled observation template

## Question and hypothesis

Define one observable change and what evidence would support or weaken the hypothesis. Do not confuse a correlated change with a mechanism.

## Conditions

Use arbitrary sample identifiers. Record water source category, sample volume, temperature, mixing/aeration state, container cleanliness, instrument check, measurement time and any intervention in a private working log. Keep a matched control. Never reconstruct exact dosing from this public template.

## Measurement

Use an appropriate calibrated instrument, fresh reference materials and a consistent rinse/reading procedure. Record stable values and uncertainty rather than selecting a single convenient number. pH, alkalinity, conductivity and dissolved oxygen are different measurements.

## Stop and review

Stop after unsafe handling conditions, instrument failure or an unplanned intervention. Do not automatically compensate for a drifting reading. Compare controls and repeats, then state limitations. This archive contains no chemical dosing, peroxide handling instructions or automated chemical actuators.
