# FDM coupon and fit worksheet

Record material family, dryness preparation, nozzle class, layer height, perimeter count, orientation, cooling strategy and enclosure condition as independent test factors. Do not assume a single successful profile transfers between printers or materials.

For each fictional sample, record nominal dimensions, measured dimensions, measurement tool, uncertainty, surface condition, leak observation where relevant, load direction and failure mode. Change one factor at a time. Repeat measurements after cooling and conditioning.

A watertight coupon is not a pressure-rated vessel, food-contact approval or long-term chemical-compatibility result. Fit allowance, creep, heat, UV and moisture can change service behavior. Keep load-bearing, mains and safety-critical use out of this generic experiment.

Use the generated fit coupon to explore clearance, not to identify any actual printer or board. Inspect mesh scale and slicer units before printing.
